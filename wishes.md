## About the table

- **Source:** YouTube auto-captions of each episode (`transcripts/txt/`). Titles and names were corrected by hand from caption spellings.
- **Ep. / Date:** season, episode number and release date from the podcast RSS feed.
- **Wished by:** the captions carry no speaker names, so each wish is attributed from the conversation around it. The 26 least certain were confirmed or corrected by ear from audio clips.
- **Format wished for:** as said on the show. "Not stated" means no format was named in the wish.
- **Format came true?** checked against US listings on Blu-ray.com. "Announced" means a dated future release; "Not yet (… listed with no date)" means Blu-ray.com has an undated entry for it under that publisher; "No" means no US release in the wished format was found.
- **Wished-for publisher?** "Yes" or "No" where the wish named a label and a release exists or is listed; "n/a" where no label was named (the actual publisher is in brackets).
- **S2E50** is the "Super Disc Wish List" special, where the whole episode is wishes; its rows are marked in Notes.
