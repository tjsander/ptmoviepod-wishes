Wishes into the Physical Media Universe

Every disc wished for at the end of Physical Therapy: A Movie Podcast, and whether the wish came true. Release status checked 13 Oct 2026.

About the show

Physical Therapy: A Movie Podcast (PT Movie Pod) is, in the show's own words, “a movie podcast dedicated to physical media, film preservation and why owning movies matters.” Hosts Kurt and Sam, with producer Kimberly and regular guests, talk through new 4K UHD and Blu-ray releases, boutique labels such as Arrow Video, Criterion, Kino Lorber and Shout Factory, and the films on their shelves.

Every episode ends with the hosts “putting wishes out into the physical media universe”: each names a film they want released on a format it isn't on yet. This page tracks all 230 wishes across 91 episodes, and the 35 that have come true so far.
